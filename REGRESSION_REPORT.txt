ERT Alpha 1.4.2 VALUATION RECOVERY

Root problem addressed:
The 1.4 forecast/DCF depended too strongly on complete current-year quarterly series and on frontend D.* annual state. A failed or incomplete refresh could therefore make the whole valuation chain unavailable.

Recovery rules:
1. Annual anchors are read directly from the validated live SEC annual object.
2. FY1 Revenue growth priority:
   comparable current YTD YoY -> TTM vs latest FY fallback -> 5Y median fallback.
3. FY1 OCF margin priority:
   current YTD OCF/Revenue -> TTM OCF/Revenue fallback -> 5Y median fallback.
4. FY1 CapEx intensity priority:
   current YTD CapEx/Revenue -> TTM CapEx/Revenue fallback -> 5Y median fallback.
5. DCF only blocks when genuinely required validated inputs (shares/net cash/WACC) are unavailable.
6. Rating no longer becomes NaN if valuation is unavailable.
7. This recovery layer only replaces the Valuation page/DCF path; Financials, Analysis, Market and Report from 1.4.1 are left intact.

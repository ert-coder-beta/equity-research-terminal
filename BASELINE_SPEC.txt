ERT BASELINE SPECIFICATION — Alpha 1.5

BL-01 Inspector stays in the right column on desktop.
BL-02 Clicking a traceable metric opens Inspector evidence.
BL-03 Inspector is not placed below the Financials table.
BL-04 One successful live snapshot is reused across page navigation.
BL-05 Page navigation does not require another manual refresh.
BL-06 Annual Financials and current-year context are one combined statement.
BL-07 No separate '2026 Current Year' tab in the baseline structure.
BL-08 Current year shows Q1/Q2/... actuals when available.
BL-09 Current year shows YTD/H1/9M actual total.
BL-10 Company Guidance stays separate from actuals.
BL-11 Analyst Consensus stays visible even when unavailable.
BL-12 ERT FY estimate stays separate from actuals/guidance/consensus.
BL-13 Fair Value must be produced by the DCF when validated required inputs exist.
BL-14 TTM is a valuation/ratio concept, not shown as a completed fiscal year.
BL-15 Report keeps Company/Business overview, business mix, strengths, risks, written analysis and charts.
BL-16 Charts supplement written analysis; they do not replace it.
BL-17 No demo consensus price target may be substituted for unavailable live consensus.
BL-18 New features must not remove or reposition baseline features without explicit approval.

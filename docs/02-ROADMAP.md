# Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Foundation
Core navigation, company switching, financial statement UI, local notes and audit model.

## Milestone 2 — SEC Data
Ticker-to-CIK mapping, Company Facts ingestion, filing metadata, XBRL mapping and annual/quarterly periods.

## Milestone 3 — Financial Analysis
Growth, margins, ROIC, ROE, ROA, FCF, SBC and capital allocation.

## Milestone 4 — Valuation
DCF, reverse DCF, scenarios, sensitivity and market-implied expectations.

## Milestone 5 — Personal MVP
Business analysis, research workflow, export, testing and bug fixing.

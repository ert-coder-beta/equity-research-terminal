# Vision

## Mission
Help long-term investors understand a business before valuing the business.

## Product principles
1. Every number traceable.
2. Every valuation explainable.
3. Understand the business before valuing the business.
4. Facts, assumptions, calculations and opinions remain distinguishable.
5. Research workflow takes priority over information density.

## Initial release path
Self-use → trusted friends → feedback → decision on wider release.
